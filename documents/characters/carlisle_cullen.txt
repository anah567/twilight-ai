CARLISLE CULLEN

Carlisle Cullen is a vampire from the Twilight saga,
written by Stephenie Meyer.

He is married to Esme Cullen and is the adoptive father
of Edward, Alice, Emmett, Rosalie, and Jasper.

Carlisle works as a doctor and is known for his compassion
toward humans.

Unlike many other vampires, Carlisle follows a diet
based on animal blood instead of human blood.

He is the founder and leader of the Cullen family.


ALICE CULLEN

Alice Cullen is a vampire and a member of the Cullen family.

She is the adoptive daughter of Carlisle and Esme Cullen.

Alice is the romantic partner of Jasper Hale.

She has the supernatural ability to see possible future events based on decisions.

Her visions can change when people make different choices.

Alice becomes a close friend of Bella Swan and helps protect her throughout the saga.

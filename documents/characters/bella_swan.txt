
BELLA SWAN

Isabella "Bella" Swan is the main protagonist of the Twilight saga, written by Stephenie Meyer.

She moves from Phoenix, Arizona, to Forks, Washington, to live with her father, Charlie Swan.

Her mother is Renée Dwyer.

Bella falls in love with Edward Cullen, a vampire.

She develops a close friendship with Jacob Black, who is a shapeshifter.

Bella eventually marries Edward Cullen, becomes a vampire, and has a daughter named Renesmee Cullen.

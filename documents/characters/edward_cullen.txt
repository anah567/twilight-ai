
EDWARD CULLEN

Edward Cullen is a vampire and one of the main characters in the Twilight saga.

He is the adoptive son of Carlisle and Esme Cullen.

Edward has the supernatural ability to read the thoughts of other people, with the exception of Bella Swan.

He falls in love with Bella Swan and eventually marries her.

Edward and Bella have a daughter named Renesmee Cullen.

Like the rest of the Cullen family, Edward avoids drinking human blood.

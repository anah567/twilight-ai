
JACOB BLACK

Jacob Black is a member of the Quileute community and one of the main characters in the Twilight saga.

He is the son of Billy Black and lives in La Push, Washington.

Jacob is a shapeshifter who can transform into a wolf.

He develops a close friendship with Bella Swan and has romantic feelings for her.

Jacob initially has a difficult relationship with Edward Cullen because of the conflict between vampires and werewolves.

Later in the saga, Jacob imprints on Renesmee Cullen.
